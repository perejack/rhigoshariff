OUR STORY

Powering the Future of Transport in Africa

Rhinggo was born from a vision to transform mobility across Africa through clean, affordable, and sustainable electric transport. We are building more than electric Mobility—we are creating an ecosystem that empowers drivers to earn more, save more, and move smarter. From innovative electric vehicles to renewable energy solutions, Rhinggo is powering a cleaner, more accessible future for mobility.

  • Lower operating costs
  • Zero tailpipe emissions
  • Smart battery technology
  • Built for African mobility
