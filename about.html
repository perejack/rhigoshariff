WHO WE ARE

Building a Cleaner, Smarter Way to Move

Rhinggo Energy Limited is a Kenyan private limited company committed to shaping the future of transport through innovative electric mobility and clean energy solutions. Incorporated under the Companies Act, 2015, the company is focused on promoting the adoption of sustainable transport by providing reliable, affordable, and environmentally responsible mobility solutions for individuals, businesses, and institutions across Kenya. Established on April 2025, Rhinggo Energy combines global innovation with local market knowledge to deliver practical solutions that respond to the changing needs of the transport sector.

RHINGGO ENERGY 01 As a subsidiary of RhingGo Technology (HK) Limited, the company draws on international technology, strategic partnerships, and industry expertise to provide world-class electric mobility products and services tailored to the Kenyan market. This strong international support enables RhingGo Energy to deliver dependable solutions that contribute to the country's transition towards cleaner and more efficient transport systems.

WHAT WE DO

More Than an Electric Vehicle

01

Electric Mobility

Rhinggo Energy supplies high-performance electric mobility designed to meet the demands of commercial transport, courier services, and personal mobility. The mobility solutions offer low operating costs, reduced maintenance requirements, and zero carbon emissions, providing a sustainable alternative to conventional petrol-powered transport.

02

Battery Swapping

Battery swapping is a core component of the Company's electric mobility ecosystem. The service enables riders and fleet operators to exchange depleted batteries for fully charged units within minutes, significantly reducing downtime and improving operational productivity.

03

Renewable Energy

Integrating solar energy and clean technology into the mobility ecosystem to create a more sustainable and cost-effective transport solution. By harnessing renewable energy, Rhinggo aims to reduce reliance on fossil fuels, lower operating costs, and support a cleaner environment while making electric mobility more accessible and reliable.
